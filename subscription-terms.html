Subscription Terms — IB EconLens

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

This page supplements Growth Education Limited's Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Refund Policy for IB EconLens subscriptions. Where this page and those policies differ on a subscription matter, this page applies.

Subscription and billing

IB EconLens is a recurring subscription: USD 9.90 per month or USD 79.20 per year, charged through Stripe. Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from the Manage subscription link on the IB EconLens website, which takes you to the Stripe Customer Portal. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period and then stops. Cancelling does not refund the current period.

Refunds

Subscription payments are generally final. We will review a refund request if it involves a duplicate payment, a checkout error, failure to receive access, materially incorrect delivery, or an accidental technical issue caused by the checkout or delivery system. Send refund review requests to info@geledtech.com within 7 calendar days of the payment, including the purchase email, order reference if available, the product name (IB EconLens), and a clear explanation of the issue. We do not refund a subscription solely because a specific academic result was not achieved.

Your data

To provide the subscription we store your email address and subscription record (customer ID, subscription ID, plan, status and current period end) in Cloudflare KV. Sign-in is by a magic link emailed to you — there is no password. Your browser stores a session token in localStorage for up to 30 days; signing out deletes it. When your subscription ends, your subscription record is deleted. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store your card number.

Students

IB EconLens is designed for IB Diploma Economics students. The subscriber is normally a parent or guardian; the student uses the service under that subscription.

Contact

Questions about subscriptions, access or refunds: info@geledtech.com.
